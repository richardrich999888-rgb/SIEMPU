## Differentiation and prior-art questions

These are review questions, not patentability conclusions:

1. Which exact step distinguishes the proposed release protocol from established ABAC, authorization services, escrow/key-release systems and zero-trust policy enforcement? Compare transaction ordering and adversary models, not product names.
2. Is binding a policy epoch, schema, suite and key identifiers to an authenticated envelope new in the proposed combination? Search published secure-message formats, cryptographic migration designs and academic constructions before claiming it.
3. Does independent custody detect a rollback that ordinary signed hash chains or transparency checkpoints miss? Specify the trusted-state assumption and show a counterexample.
4. Which intermittent-connectivity invariant is achieved without an online authority? State the availability/freshness trade-off and bounded offline disclosure explicitly.
5. Is provider-neutral hardware custody implemented across independently developed devices, or only a single software backend? Interoperability and qualification need separate evidence.
6. Does sovereign operation mean customer-controlled deployment, update signing, directory, policy and key custody? These are testable properties, not proof of domestic origin for every dependency.

The initial differentiation target is a reproducible demonstration of current-policy release and independently verifiable evidence across explicit cryptographic profiles. No search here establishes legal novelty or freedom to operate; a qualified prior-art/IP review remains open.
