Initial implementation of endpoint-encrypted text/file exchange, mandatory TOTP, approved software-device binding, unit/mission policy, encrypted offline queue and policy-epoch-bound recipient release.

Includes independent evidence verification, administrator console, blind ciphertext relay, versioned SQLite migrations, encrypted backup/restore, controlled offline identity recovery, tests, source-linked documentation and artifact-only CI release packaging.

This version is a prototype. It does not claim operational IAF/SAG approval, hardware attestation, forward secrecy, cross-domain enforcement, multi-host authority availability, public-network deployment or a patent grant. The tested behavior and remaining scope are recorded in `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`, `docs/EXECUTION_CHECKLIST.md` and the validation/CI artifacts for each revision.
