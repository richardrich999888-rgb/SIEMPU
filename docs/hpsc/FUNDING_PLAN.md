## Why grant funding is needed

SIEPMU is not being built from nothing. A tested laboratory prototype already exists, at provisional
TRL 4 (`docs/trl/TRL_ASSESSMENT.md`). It covers:

- endpoint E2EE, MFA and device binding;
- policy-epoch-bound release with signed evidence;
- an independent verifier, the relay and the offline queue;
- a synthetic integration adapter and monitoring;
- hardened deployment, all with automated and scripted evidence.

What a startup cannot fund alone is the move from laboratory evidence to **validated,
representative and assessed** evidence. That move needs:

- a sponsor-agreed relevant environment and a multi-host representative deployment;
- hardware trust (TPM/FIDO2/HSM trials);
- qualification of an approved Indian cryptographic provider;
- independent security and cryptographic review;
- witnessed trials.

The grant buys down these technology and integration risks.

### Reading the model

- **Grant cap.** At the estimated PDB of about ₹1.62 cr, the 50 % rule caps the grant at about
  ₹0.81 cr, not ₹1.5 cr.
- **Reaching ₹1.5 cr.** The full ceiling needs a PDB of at least ₹3.0 cr, with a matching
  contribution of at least ₹1.5 cr. That needs either a larger funded scope or documented past
  expenditure and in-kind contribution. **Founder decision.**
- **Matching contribution.** It can be cash, past expenditure or in-kind (rule G3). IP counts for
  at most the lower of 20 % of the MC or ₹25 lakh (G4). The model reports
  `declaredMatchingCoversMinimum=FAIL` until the founder enters actual figures.
- **Spend rules.** Cost overruns are not grant-eligible (G7), so the plan carries no hidden
  contingency.

## Funding narrative: technology de-risking, not a contract

The sequence: existing engineering prototype → grant-supported validation → reduced technology and
integration risk → stronger defence adoption pathway → better investor readiness.

- **Not a commitment.** A grant is not a purchase order, investment, procurement commitment or
  deployment authorisation.
- **Traction that may be stated once documented:** confirmed advisers, formal incubation support,
  filed IP applications, verified demonstrations, investor evaluations, confirmed partnerships.
  **Founder to supply evidence for each.** Until then the deck leaves these fields marked.
